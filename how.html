How it works

A simulator, not a vault.

1

Pick an amount and a split

Choose how much goes to dollars and how much to stocks. Nothing leaves your wallet; there is no wallet.

2

Dollars earn a rate

We take each yield's 30-day rate and show what it pays in a year if the rate holds, next to the 3-month T-bill. Rates change every day.

3

Stocks move with the share

We show how many tokens your money buys at today's on-chain price, how far the token sits from the real share, and how it moved over the past week.

Before you move real money

  • A rate above the T-bill is paid for with risk: smart-contract bugs, lending defaults, or a synthetic dollar losing its peg.
  • Tokenized T-bill funds often limit who can hold them. Most are closed to US retail.
  • Robinhood stock tokens are debt securities from a Jersey company, not shares, and are not for US persons or UK residents.
  • Past moves are not future moves. This is a simulator with live numbers, not financial advice.
